ABOUT THE ASSOCIATION

BUILDING A STRONGER PARA-SPORTING COMMUNITY

Punjab Para Sports Association works to expand sporting opportunities for athletes across Punjab through participation, competition and structured sporting development.

Governing 23 districts under the leadership of state sports administrators, PPSA provides talent scouting, scientific classification, equipment assistance, and accredited competition pathways that carry Punjab's athletes from grassroots trials to national and Paralympic podiums.

Track & Field

Para Athletics

Track sprints, middle distance, long distance, and field throwing disciplines across seated (F51-57) and ambulant categories.

Key Events: 100m, 200m, 400m, Discus, Club Throw, Shot Put, Javelin
Strength & Bench

Power Lifting

Bench press competition testing maximum upper body power across 10 Men's and 10 Women's standardized bodyweight divisions.

Categories: Up to 49kg to +107kg (Men) | Up to 41kg to +86kg (Women)
Racquet Sport

Para Badminton

Fast-paced court sport spanning wheelchair classes (WH1, WH2), standing lower/upper limb (SL3, SL4, SU5) and short stature (SS6).

Events: Singles, Doubles, and Mixed Doubles competitions
Team Championship

Wheel-Chair Basketball

High-intensity team tournament played on custom sport wheelchairs, utilizing functional classification points (1.0 to 4.5).

Tournament: Men's & Women's District Team Championships
ORGANIZATIONAL MISSION

MORE THAN SPORT

PPSA operates with a deep commitment to athletic parity, dignifying disability through competitive sporting pathways and state-level representation.

01

OPPORTUNITY

Creating pathways for athletes to participate regardless of geographical location, economic background, or classification category across all districts of Punjab.

02

COMPETITION

Providing structured sporting opportunities through official district trials, state championships, accredited timing gates, and strict compliance with national technical standards.

03

REPRESENTATION

Supporting athletes through the sporting ecosystem by facilitating Paralympic Committee of India accreditation, government sports quota advocacy, and international exposure.
